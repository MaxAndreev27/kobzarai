# Code of Conduct

We want KobzarAI to be a welcoming and respectful community. Participants are
expected to communicate constructively, respect differing viewpoints, accept
feedback in good faith, and protect other people's privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, publication of
private information without consent, and other abusive conduct are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may remove content, request changes, or restrict participation
when conduct violates these standards. They will review reports as fairly and
discreetly as practical.

To report a concern, contact [@MaxAndreev27](https://github.com/MaxAndreev27)
through an available private channel. If no private channel is available,
open an issue only to request one; do not post sensitive details publicly.
